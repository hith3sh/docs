## How the free limit works

- The free plan includes **one connected app**.
- Billing is based on **distinct integration slugs**, not total connection rows.
- Once you already have one distinct app connected, adding a different app prompts you to upgrade.
- Your existing connected app keeps working even if you do not upgrade.
- Pro removes that one-app cap so you can keep connecting more services.

If you connect two Gmail accounts, that is still one distinct integration for billing. If you connect Gmail and Slack, that counts as two.

ClawLink currently gates billing by connected integrations, not by monthly request quotas.

## What you see on the Billing page

In **Dashboard > Billing**, ClawLink shows:

- Your current plan and billing status
- How many distinct integrations are connected
- Whether the next new integration requires an upgrade
- Your renewal date when Pro is active
- A direct upgrade or **Manage subscription** action

If you return from checkout and still see the free plan for a moment, wait a minute and refresh. Billing state is synced after checkout completes and the webhook lands.

## Upgrading to Pro

To upgrade:

1. Go to **Dashboard > Billing**
2. Click **Upgrade to Pro**
3. Complete checkout in Polar
4. Return to the dashboard and refresh if needed

Your paid plan becomes active as soon as checkout and billing sync finish.

## Managing your subscription

If you already have Pro, use **Manage subscription** on the Billing page to:

- Update your payment method
- View invoices and receipts
- Cancel renewal for a future billing period

ClawLink sends you to the Polar customer portal for those actions.

## Billing FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a credit card to start?">
    No. You can connect your first app on the free plan without adding a card. A payment method is only required when you upgrade to Pro.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I try to connect a second app on Free?">
    ClawLink blocks the new connection and sends you to Billing to upgrade. Your existing connected app stays active.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I manage or cancel my subscription later?">
    Yes. Once you're on Pro, open **Dashboard > Billing** and click **Manage subscription** to open the Polar customer portal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does reconnecting the same provider count as another app?">
    No. Billing is based on distinct integrations, not total connection rows. Reconnecting Gmail or adding another Gmail account does not require a new upgrade. Adding a different integration like Slack does.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much is Pro?">
    ClawLink Pro is **$5/month**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
