---
title: "Social Media"
description: "YouTube, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and Instagram integrations"
---

Connect your social accounts so your agent can publish content, manage your presence, and keep your channels active — without logging into each platform manually.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="YouTube">
    YouTube lets your agent upload videos and manage your channel.

    **What you can do:**
    - Upload videos with titles, descriptions, and tags
    - Update video metadata
    - Manage playlists
    - Retrieve channel and video analytics

    **Credentials:** OAuth 2.0 — authorize access through your Google account.

    **Example prompt:**
    > "Upload this week's tutorial video to my YouTube channel with the title 'Getting Started with ClawLink' and set it to Public."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Twitter / X">
    Twitter/X lets your agent post tweets and manage your account.

    **What you can do:**
    - Post tweets and threads
    - Reply to and retweet posts
    - Fetch your timeline or search results
    - Manage your following list

    **Credentials:** OAuth 2.0 — connect your Twitter/X account through the authorization flow. You'll need a Twitter Developer account with a project and app set up.

    **Example prompt:**
    > "Draft and post a tweet announcing our new feature launch. Keep it under 280 characters and use the hashtag #ClawLink."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="LinkedIn">
    LinkedIn lets your agent post updates and manage connections on your profile or company page.

    **What you can do:**
    - Post text and media updates to your profile or company page
    - Manage connection requests
    - Retrieve profile and engagement data

    **Credentials:** OAuth 2.0 — authorize access through your LinkedIn account.

    **Example prompt:**
    > "Post a LinkedIn update sharing our latest blog post about AI automation with a short summary."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Instagram">
    Instagram lets your agent publish posts and manage media on your business account.

    **What you can do:**
    - Publish photo and carousel posts
    - Manage captions and hashtags
    - Retrieve post performance metrics

    **Credentials:** OAuth 2.0 via Meta — connect your Instagram Business account through Facebook Login. Your account must be a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page.

    **Example prompt:**
    > "Schedule an Instagram post for tomorrow at 9 AM with the product photo and a caption highlighting this week's sale."
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
