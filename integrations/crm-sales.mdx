---
title: "CRM & Sales"
description: "Apollo, HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pipedrive integrations"
---

Connect your CRM and sales tools so your agent can manage leads, update deals, and keep your pipeline moving — without manual data entry.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Apollo">
    Apollo lets your agent search for leads and manage contacts in your Apollo workspace.

    **What you can do:**
    - Search for prospects by job title, company, location, and more
    - View and update contact details
    - Add contacts to sequences

    **Credentials:** API key — find it in Apollo under **Settings → Integrations → API**.

    **Example prompt:**
    > "Find 10 VP of Engineering contacts at Series B SaaS companies in New York using Apollo."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="HubSpot">
    HubSpot lets your agent manage contacts, deals, and pipelines in your CRM.

    **What you can do:**
    - Create and update contacts and companies
    - Open and move deals through pipeline stages
    - Log notes and activities
    - Query CRM records

    **Credentials:** Private app token — create a private app in HubSpot under **Settings → Integrations → Private Apps** and grant the scopes you need.

    **Example prompt:**
    > "Create a new deal in HubSpot for Acme Corp worth $12,000 and move it to the Proposal Sent stage."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Salesforce">
    Salesforce lets your agent manage CRM data and workflows across your org.

    **What you can do:**
    - Create, read, update, and delete standard and custom objects
    - Query records using SOQL
    - Manage leads, opportunities, and accounts
    - Trigger flows and workflows

    **Credentials:** OAuth 2.0 — connect via the Salesforce authorization flow using your org credentials.

    **Example prompt:**
    > "Update the Opportunity stage for TechCorp to 'Closed Won' in Salesforce and set the close date to today."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pipedrive">
    Pipedrive lets your agent manage deals and sales pipelines in your account.

    **What you can do:**
    - Create and update deals
    - Add and manage contacts and organizations
    - Move deals between stages
    - Log activities and notes

    **Credentials:** API token — find it in Pipedrive under **Settings → Personal preferences → API**.

    **Example prompt:**
    > "Add a new deal in Pipedrive for the lead I just spoke with: Jane Smith at GlobalRetail, deal value $8,500."
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
