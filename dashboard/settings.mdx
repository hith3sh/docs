title: "Settings"
description: "Manage your account, API keys, billing, and notifications"
---

The Settings page lets you control everything about your ClawLink account. Use the sidebar to switch between the five tabs: Profile, API Keys, Billing, Notifications, and Security.

## Profile

The Profile tab shows your name, email address, and avatar.

- **Name** — edit your display name and click **Save Changes** to update it.
- **Email** — your email address is set by your login provider and cannot be changed here.
- **Avatar** — click **Change Avatar** to upload a profile picture.

## API keys

Your API key is what ClawLink uses to authenticate requests from your agent. You'll find your live key on this tab.

To copy your key, click the **Copy** button next to the masked key value. The button confirms when the key is in your clipboard.

To generate a new key, click **+ Create New Key**.

<Warning>
Your live API key grants full access to your ClawLink account. Do not share it in public repositories, client-side code, or anywhere others can see it. If a key is compromised, generate a new one immediately.
</Warning>

## Billing

The Billing tab shows your current plan, billing status, and whether your next app connection requires an upgrade.

**Free plan** includes:
- 1 connected app
- No credit card required

**ClawLink Pro** costs **$5/month** and unlocks additional app connections.

The Billing tab also shows:

- Your connected app count
- Whether you can keep connecting apps on your current plan
- Your renewal date if Pro is active

To upgrade, click **Upgrade to Pro**. If you already subscribe, click **Manage subscription** to open the Polar customer portal.

## Notifications

The Notifications tab lets you enable or disable each type of alert.

| Notification | When it fires |
|---|---|
| **Request failures** | An API call returns an error |
| **Rate limit warnings** | You're approaching your monthly request limit |
| **Integration disconnections** | An integration loses its connection or credentials expire |
| **Usage alerts** | Your usage exceeds 80% of your monthly limit |
| **Product updates** | News about new features and integrations |

Toggle each notification on or off using the switch next to it.

## Security

The Security tab gives you control over your account access.

- **Password** — click **Change** to update your password.
- **Two-factor authentication** — click **Enable** to add a second verification step when you log in. This is strongly recommended.
- **Active sessions** — click **View** to see all devices currently logged in to your account. You can revoke any session you don't recognize.
